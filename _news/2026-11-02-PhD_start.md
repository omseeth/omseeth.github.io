I'm starting a PhD in Computer Science at the [Hasso Plattner Institute](https://hpi.de/), joining [Paul Röttger](https://paulrottger.com/)'s new AI Safety lab following his move from Oxford.

We're ambitious and aim to establish the lab as a new lighthouse for AI safety research in Berlin and Brandenburg. My research will explore a range of topics, including AI agents, collusion, misalignment, cybersecurity, chain-of-thought and action monitoring, as well as model evaluation.

I'd love to connect with others working on these or related topics!
