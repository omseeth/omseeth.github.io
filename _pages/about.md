By training, I'm a philosopher, computer scientist, and linguist with a focus on NLP, ML, safety, and ethics. I hold a B.Sc. and an M.Sc. in Computational Linguistics and Computer Science, as well as a B.A. and an M.A. in Philosophy. I'm currently pursuing a PhD in Computer Science under the supervision of [Paul Röttger](https://paulrottger.com/) at the [Hasso Plattner Institute](https://hpi.de/) in Germany.

My research will explore a range of topics, including AI agents, collusion, misalignment, cybersecurity, chain-of-thought and action monitoring, as well as model evaluation.

My translation of the French title [*Le pouvoir de l’art*](https://editions-saintsimon.fr/livres/pouvoir-de-lart/) can be found here: [*Die Macht der Kunst*](https://www.merve.de/index.php/book/show/460).
